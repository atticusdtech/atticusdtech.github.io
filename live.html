Live help desk

I run the help desk with ITIL. An incident is something that broke for someone. When the same thing keeps breaking, I open a problem to find the root cause. The fix goes out as a planned change, and related changes ship together as a release. This page follows that chain, updated each school day from the service desk.

Only finished incidents and solved problems are shown. Titles are rewritten to leave out people, places and anything security-sensitive.
