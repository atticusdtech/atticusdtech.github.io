How the numbers are made

The ticketing system records first response, assignment and resolution times on every ticket, in business hours. A script pulls my tickets through its API and computes medians and shares. It publishes only the totals on this page.

The help desk has two SLA rules, and both are alarms rather than targets. One escalates a ticket that sits unassigned for two hours, the other a ticket that goes two days without a comment. The compliance figure is the share of tickets that never tripped either.

Summer is left out of the headline figures. In June and July I inherited a backlog of older tickets, and those times reflect the backlog, not the work.
